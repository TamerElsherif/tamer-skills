> Built from: <recordings, total talk time>. Version <n>, <date>. Numbers: metrics.md. Evidence: corpus.md.
> This describes how <Speaker> talks. It is not a list of corrections. The coaching skill uses it to keep the voice.

## 1. Fingerprint (one paragraph)
How it sounds to a listener in 4–5 plain sentences: pace, energy, language mix, how ideas are built, the feeling
it leaves. Write it so a speechwriter could draft in this voice after reading only this paragraph.

## 3. Signature moves (keep these)
5–10 moves, each: name · what it does · 1–2 verbatim quotes with [recording m:ss] · how often.
Look for: how a point opens (question, story, claim, number), how it's explained (analogy, example from
work, contrast «مش X، ده Y»), how it lands (one-line takeaway, callback, humor), how the speaker handles others
(builds on them, summarizes, challenges, hands over).

## 4. Vocabulary
- Signature words and phrases (from the distinctive list, checked in the corpus: not transcription noise).
- English: which terms stay English, which get Arabized («الـ Product»), where in the sentence English sits.
- Register: Egyptian colloquial / MSA moments / professional jargon; humor and idioms.

## 5. How an explanation is built
The usual skeleton, with one full example from the corpus (quoted, with timestamp), e.g.
"claim → why it matters for the business → real example from his work → one-line takeaway".

## 6. Delivery
Pace changes, where pauses fall (before the key word? while searching?), emphasis, energy at the start vs the
end of a turn. Only what the numbers or the corpus support.

## 7. Habits to polish (optional, never to remove the voice)
2–5 items, each with the measured rate and a light fix that keeps the style, e.g. "«يعني» 31/1000 words,
mostly at turn starts: drop the first one, keep the mid-sentence ones". Mark each: polish / leave alone.

## 8. Authenticity guardrails
What a coach, writer or AI must NOT change: the moves in §3, the language mix, the humor, the register.
What NOT to imitate from generic "public speaking" advice that would make this speaker sound unlike himself.

## 9. Open questions
What the recordings can't show (e.g. no solo stage talk yet, only conversation) and what to record next.
