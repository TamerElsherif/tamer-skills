## s03_rules | rules | O1
template: bullets
narrator: Four rules come before anything else. ^ Keep an itemised receipt for every expense over twenty-five dollars. A card statement alone doesn't count. ^ Submit within thirty days of the expense. ^ After ninety days, a claim isn't paid at all. ^ And personal costs, like family meals, are never business expenses.
claim: A receipt is required for every expense over $25. [S01#c1]
claim: A card statement alone is not a receipt. [S01#c1]
claim: Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date. [S01#c1]
claim: Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. [S01#c1]
claim: Personal costs such as family meals must not be submitted as business expenses. [S01#c1]
