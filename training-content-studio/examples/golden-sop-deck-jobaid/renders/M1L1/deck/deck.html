Four rules before you start

  • Itemised receipt for every expense over $25A card statement alone is not a receipt
  • Submit within 30 days
  • After 90 days: not reimbursed
  • Personal costs are never business expenses

Speaker notes

Narrator Four rules come before anything else. Keep an itemised receipt for every expense over twenty-five dollars. A card statement alone doesn't count. Submit within thirty days of the expense. After ninety days, a claim isn't paid at all. And personal costs, like family meals, are never business expenses.

If asked about small items: no receipt needed at $25 or less, but keeping one never hurts.

Speaker notes

Narrator When you travel, these limits apply. Meals are sixty dollars a day at home, and eighty-five dollars abroad. Hotels are capped at two hundred and twenty dollars a night, unless the Travel Desk approves an exception in writing. Flights under six hours are economy. And alcohol is only paid for client entertainment your manager approved in advance.

Example (SOP §8): Mona's pre-approved $140 client dinner incl. wine was reimbursed in full.

Narrator Now, the claim itself, in six steps. Photograph the receipt on the same day. Open the Northwind Expenses app and select New Claim. Choose the category, then enter the date, amount and business purpose. Attach the receipt photo to each line, and check the total. Submit it to your approver within thirty days. And answer any question from your approver within five business days.

Never do this

Never

  • Split one expense to stay under a limit
  • Claim personal costs or upgrades
  • Inflate a claim (misconduct)

Instead

  • One expense, one claim, real amount
  • Unsure? Ask the Finance Help Desk

Speaker notes

Narrator Three things you must never do. Never split one expense into several claims to stay under a limit. Never submit personal costs or private upgrades. And never inflate a claim. False claims are treated as misconduct, and can lead to dismissal. Instead, submit each expense once, with its real amount, and ask Finance when you're unsure.

Stress: false or inflated claims can lead to dismissal (§6).
