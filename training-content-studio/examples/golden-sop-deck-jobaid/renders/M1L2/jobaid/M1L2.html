Expense claim checklist

Northwind Co. Expense Policy SOP-FIN-007 v3.2 · effective 2026-03-01

Before you press Submit

  • Itemised receipt for every expense over $25
  • Receipt photo attached to each line
  • Total matches the receipts
  • Category, date, amount and purpose entered
  • Submitted within 30 days of the expense
  • Within meal and hotel limits
  • No personal costs included

Limits and approvers

ItemRule
Meals (per diem)$60/day domestic · $85/day international
HotelMax $220/night (Travel Desk exception in writing)
Flights under 6 hEconomy class
AlcoholOnly pre-approved client entertainment
ApproverManager ≤ $1,000 · Director ≤ $5,000 · CFO > $5,000

Never / instead

Never

  • Split one expense to stay under a limit
  • Claim family meals or private upgrades
  • Inflate a claim: it is misconduct

Instead

  • One expense, one claim, real amount
  • Unsure? Ask before you submit
