# Northwind Co. Data Retention Policy (POL-IT-003)

Version: 1.4
Effective date: 2019-05-10
Owner: IT & Legal
Last reviewed: 2019-05-10

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how long Northwind Co. keeps business records and how it disposes of them safely.

## 2. Retention periods

1. Financial records, including expense receipts, are retained for 6 years.
2. Employee personnel files are retained for 5 years after the employee leaves.
3. Customer contracts are retained for 10 years after the contract ends.
4. Routine email is retained for 2 years unless it is subject to a legal hold.

## 3. Disposal

- Paper records must be shredded with a cross-cut shredder or placed in a locked confidential waste bin.
- Never throw records that contain personal data into an ordinary recycling bin.
- Electronic records must be deleted using the IT-approved secure deletion tool.

## 4. Legal hold

When Legal issues a legal hold, the affected records must not be deleted, even if their retention period has ended.
